DEVICE CHECKS

Check this browser.

Two quick checks that run on this computer: whether it can unlock with a passkey, and whether the packaged encryption components work. A third, experimental one asks two Quilibrium services whether instant sync would be possible. None of them touches your vault. Backups, restore and reset are on the Backup & restore page.

THIS DEVICE ONLY

Passkey check

Check whether this browser can unlock QVault with a passkey, for example Touch ID. You will be asked to confirm three times: once to create a test passkey named “QVault passkey check” and twice to use it. Nothing is saved in QVault and no cloud service is contacted by QVault. You can delete the test passkey afterwards in your password manager.

EXPERIMENTAL · CONTACTS QPING AND QQ

Instant sync check

QVault currently looks for changes from your other computers every 20 seconds. This check finds out whether your account could do it at once instead: it creates one temporary topic in QPing and one temporary queue in QQ, sends a single test message through them and removes both again. Nothing from your vault is sent. The browser asks for permission to contact the two services; the access details are used for this check only and are not saved.
